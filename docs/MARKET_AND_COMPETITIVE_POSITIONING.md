# Pakistan Market and Competitive Positioning

## Initial market focus

Target small and medium regional fibre and wireless ISPs operating in Pakistan. The project should not assume that every licensed operator is active, addressable or a suitable prospect.

## Customer problem hypothesis

Regional ISPs may have fragmented tools for subscriber management, billing, payments, network authentication, customer communication and field operations. Some may have an established backend they cannot or do not want to replace; others may lack a capable integrated system.

These are hypotheses to validate through interviews and prospect research, not universal claims about Pakistani ISPs.

## Positioning hypothesis

A hybrid platform that can:
- Provide native management where an ISP needs it.
- Integrate with existing systems where an ISP wants to retain them.
- Offer a consistent subscriber self-service and payment experience.
- Connect through modular adapters and a secure local Bridge.

## Competitive landscape to investigate

Examples previously raised for market comparison include Sonar, Splynx, ISPbox and UISP CRM. Their current features, pricing, Pakistan presence, integration capabilities and customer adoption must be independently verified before using them in sales claims or strategy.

## Research plan

Build a verified prospect database with:
- ISP/company name and operating districts.
- Public business contact details.
- PTA licence category/status where verifiable.
- Access technology (fibre, wireless or other) where verifiable.
- Approximate subscriber scale only when supported by credible evidence.
- Existing billing/management platform only when verifiable.
- Likely integration fit and evidence.
- Research date and confidence/source notes.

Start with a manageable first cohort of promising regional ISPs. Do not invent contacts, subscriber counts or software usage.

## Go-to-market questions

- Which problems are painful enough that ISPs will pay to solve them?
- Which existing backends are most common in the target segment?
- Which payment methods are operationally important?
- What onboarding and support model is affordable?
- What is the smallest useful pilot scope?
- Which integration provides the greatest adoption leverage?

## Guardrails

Do not claim market size, active ISP counts, competitor superiority or compatibility without dated, credible evidence. Separate licence counts from active unique providers and avoid adding overlapping licence categories.
