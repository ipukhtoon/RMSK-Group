# Product Vision

## Vision

Build a dependable, Pakistan-first SaaS platform that helps small and medium ISPs manage subscriber-facing services and operational workflows without forcing every ISP onto one vendor's network equipment or existing backend.

## Problem

ISPs differ in size, network design, upstream providers, routers, authentication systems, fibre/OLT/ONU equipment, payment practices and existing billing/management software. A rigid product that requires every ISP to replace its backend creates adoption friction and migration risk.

## Product promise

Offer one coherent platform with three complementary modes:

1. **Customer self-service:** subscribers can view relevant account/service information, understand balances and payments, and use supported self-service actions.
2. **Optional native ISP management:** ISPs without a suitable backend may use the platform's own operational capabilities.
3. **Integration-first mode:** ISPs with an existing backend can retain it and connect through supported adapters and RMSK Bridge.

## Principles

- Pakistan-first product and go-to-market.
- Backend-agnostic and vendor-agnostic integration strategy.
- Modular adapters; no universal compatibility claims before validation.
- Multi-tenant SaaS with strict server-side tenant isolation.
- Secure-by-default payment, identity, device and integration design.
- Reliable operation during intermittent connectivity.
- Clear auditability and operational visibility.
- API-first design where it provides real integration value.
- Documentation-first, owner-approved delivery.

## Initial customer segment

Small and medium regional fibre and wireless ISPs in Pakistan. Mobile network operators and very large national operators are not the initial target segment.

## Success measures to define

Before launch, establish measurable targets for onboarding time, subscriber/account sync accuracy, payment reconciliation, Bridge availability, integration recovery, support burden, tenant-isolation tests and customer adoption. Numeric targets remain to be approved.

## Out of scope until approved

- A claim that every router, OLT, ONU, RADIUS server or billing platform is supported.
- Forced replacement of an ISP's existing backend.
- Unverified payment gateway or hardware compatibility claims.
- International expansion as a launch requirement.
- Any implementation not explicitly approved by the owner.
