# UX and Branding

## Company identity

- Approved working company brand: **RMSK Group**.
- No separate product name has been approved.
- Do not use former naming candidates as the current project identity.
- Company registration, trademark clearance and domain ownership are pending/unverified.

## Market and language

Pakistan is the initial market. Plan for local ISP workflows, local currency/payment practices and appropriate Urdu/English language support. Exact languages, localization priorities and terminology require product design approval.

## Experience principles

- Responsive, mobile-first customer and staff experiences.
- Clear, low-friction subscriber self-service.
- Efficient workflows for small ISP teams.
- Accessible typography, contrast, touch targets and keyboard behavior.
- Consistent design system and reusable components.
- Clear loading, empty, error, offline and pending states.
- Avoid hiding critical account/payment/service state.
- Show integration health and explain actionable failures.
- Tenant-specific branding without compromising platform security.

## White-label direction

Allow tenant branding and customer-facing identity configuration where approved. White-label controls must not permit one tenant to access another tenant's assets or settings.

## Open decisions

Product name, logo, colors, typography, design system, exact screens, notification tone and localization scope have not been approved.
